# Art Production Pack v0.1
## Direction
Original fictional characters; gritty ink outlines, halftone, worn paper, warm streetlight, charcoal #171916, red #A23B36, cream #E3C58A. Avoid real brand logos and copied characters. Reference images are in references/. Replace dollar balance and wager controls shown in mockups with match score and turn controls.

## Layout
Portrait logical canvas 360x640, responsive safe areas. Top score and opponent; center characters and playing surface; bottom rules/status and large roll button. Minimum touch target 44 CSS px. UI text separate from art. Keep contrast high. No essential text baked into background. Use placeholder art until assets arrive; never crop mockups into fake production sprites.
## Deliverables
Home, practice, point state, round result, session result, rules overlay. Later: lobby, location selection, customization. Art QA: silhouette, consistent proportions, transparent edges, no stray logos, dice faces correct. Actual separated production art remains to be generated.
